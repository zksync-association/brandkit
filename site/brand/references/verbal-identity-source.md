## Brand Narrative
At regular intervals throughout history, new technological advancements have radically increased individual freedom, creating a flywheel that accelerates creativity, innovation, and prosperity. We're in the midst of one of these transformations now.

As we advance through the Information Age, our structures of accounting, governance, record keeping and communication are all in need of a system upgrade.

The centralized forces of big tech, finance, and government claim to act as counterweights, balancing each others' power and protecting the rights of the individuals within their borders, but they often act in lockstep, further centralizing data, money, surveillance and control.

We once felt powerless to change the slow, corrupt systems we inherited, but cryptography gave us the keys to our freedom. We have new tools to protect our privacy, coordinate resources, resist censorship, spread memetic ideas, and build ambitious projects. And we don't need to ask anyone for permission to innovate.

We aim to build trustless systems, networks of machines bound by mathematics, infallible to corruption unlike their human creators. But machines do not run in isolation. We need a decentralized community to coordinate, steward, and protect the power and potential of our technology.

ZK Nation aims to coordinate this community to collectively govern and evolve the ZKsync network.

Together, we aim to build a new digital nation that transcends physical borders, with a shared mission to accelerate freedom, progress and prosperity for all.

### Brand Narrative — Intentional Innovation
Our industry is full of opportunity, but it is also full of excess. Anyone building new systems, products, or communities here for more than a few years has become accustomed to seeing mind-blowing sums of money thrown around, and often thrown away.

We have high ambitions for what we can achieve together in the coming decades, and even centuries. We aim to build resilient systems that last long after the boundaries that define current nation states fall away. We have all of the resources we need to succeed, but we need new models for distribution and execution.

Attempts at innovation in crypto often feel like throwing expensive darts at a moving target. Once in a while we hit the target, but it's quickly forgotten in the chase for a shiny new narrative or token. To get us to the future we envision, we're proposing a different approach.

Note: to replace indefinite and definite optimism, we recommend the following framework:

### Brand Narrative — Zk/Acc
We are in an age of unprecedented technological acceleration. From here it will only continue to gain speed.

The combination of technology and open markets is often referred to as the techno-capital machine, a self-perpetuating engine of innovation and abundance. This machine is ideally meritocratic: it rewards the most productive ideas and creations, continuing the cycle of endless growth and acceleration.

Recent discussions of accelerationism are primarily focused on artificial intelligence. Rapid advances in AI have created a flywheel for increased centralization, with more and more data and financial resources funneled into a few organizations. These organizations aim to become all-knowing, all-seeing powers: constantly surveilling and scraping data from the internet to build godlike intelligence.

Some argue that we should decelerate, take our foot off the gas, and slow down our rapid progress. This impulse will only lead to further centralization. The wheels of the techno-capital machine will keep turning. If we slow it down in one place, it will continue to accelerate elsewhere.

There is a better way to bring the machine into balance, by harnessing an equal but opposite force for decentralization. Blockchains provide a powerful counterweight to centralized power. These decentralized networks create locked open markets, increasing financial access and sovereignty by allowing individuals to own their wealth, data, and identity independently.

Blockchains enable anyone to fund innovation beyond established power structures, accelerating decentralization and rewarding progress from the edges.

At present, Ethereum comes closest to realizing this vision. It stands as a trustless, secure, reliable, censorship-resistant, and sovereign network. However, in its current form it can't scale to accommodate endless acceleration, and it does not allow for native privacy to protect its users from surveillance.

Zero knowledge proofs uniquely enable decentralized acceleration. ZK, endearingly dubbed "Magic Moon Math," is a marvel of technology. It enables scaling and privacy on Ethereum by allowing individuals to selectively share the minimum data required to take action.

To use most services on the internet today, we trade our private information for use as training data for powerful AI. ZK can preserve our privacy. With ZK, we can send funds to family around the world without revealing the total balance of our accounts to a bank or corrupt government. We can confirm that we are a member of a nation or network state in order to place a vote and voice our opinion, without the risk of revealing our specific identity.

Continued centralization of power can only lead to continued corruption. We can't trust these powers to be keepers of the world's knowledge. The techno-capital machine can accelerate freedom, prosperity and progress for every sovereign individual. For this vision to become real, ZK is the endgame.

### Brand Narrative — On Digital Nations
This is the founding of a digital nation.

We are built to support connection and communication, and safeguard interaction from censorship and surveillance. We are anti-static: we adhere to mobility.

We affirm the right to digital assembly and fruitful organization – no permission required.

We believe in fairness and equitability.

We have a right to privacy and consent: everyone here controls their own digital footprint. Personal data is sacrosanct. We are as anonymous as we want to be.

Our fellow members assert and uphold each other's sovereignty over their own lives, destinies, assets, and exchanges. We affirm agency and prosperity, and ban interference and coercion.

We are a collective of co-educators. We promote digital literacy and share our access to learning and tools.

We celebrate the right to exit: each one of us is free to pursue our own path to self-determination.

We celebrate transparency and accountability, fairness and equitability.

We know and reject what the modern nation became: a hostile territory bound up with identity, unity, centrality, enforced by physical borders and controlled by a corrupt few.

Advancements in technology have fallen short of their proponents' promises to emancipate the collective from geographic, genealogical, and geopolitical constraints.

Efforts to decentralize have quickly calcified into unwieldy bureaucracies, or worse, splintered their advocate constituents into disparate groups in conflict not only with each other, but with their own foundational ideologies. Our digital nation redresses these failures.

The word "nation" shares a root with "nascent"; in Latin, nasci means "to be born." We know that associating national membership with lineage or birthplace is the most dangerous part of the old nationalist arsenal – a means of exclusion, persecution, the worst of all things. But our digital nation cannot be born into. It cannot itself be born, either. The digital nation must itself be nascent, continually. Its tenets can never be fixed, never predetermined by its lineage or territorial lines, but re-programmable, renewable, and emerging, always.

As a space of constant adaptation, the digital nation is inherently international, transnational, supranational. It implies its own plural: a digital nation is a nation of nations. It is also antinationalist: a space immune to the corrosive "ism" that is the institution.

We embrace union but refuse homogeneity. We are consenting, mutually accepting "others" – which means than [sic] no one can be "othered." In the digital nation there is no erasure (unless you want to be erased). If there are no margins, there can be no space of exclusion.

We do not belong to the digital nation, because the digital nation belongs to us. We are bound by choice. We govern ourselves – not each other – but we help each other to do it. We are centrifugal, but not divided. We are a universe, not a monolith.

### Brand Narrative — On Freedom
Accelerating and increasing individual freedom is core to our mission. Freedom from control and coercion, and towards self-determination. We are committed to defending the following rights and freedoms:

- **Freedom of Network:** The right to choose, participate in, and move between various online platforms, protocols, or networks without undue hindrance.
- **Freedom of Communication:** The ability to write, share, and implement software without restriction, crucial for the development of new cryptographic methods and technologies.
- **Right to Assembly:** The freedom to come together and communicate online, establish digital communities, and collaborate. Freedom to speak, publish, and transact freely without interference from a centralized authority.
- **Right to Be Forgotten:** The option to remove or transfer one's personal data and digital artifacts, enabling users to control their online legacy.
- **Right to Privacy:** The protection of one's personal data from unauthorized access and the assurance of secure, private communications.
- **Right to Transparency:** Ensuring that the rules and operations of the system itself are transparent and auditable by its users.
- **Right to Property and Sovereignty:** The ability to own, control, and transfer digital assets, respecting the same principles that govern physical property rights.
- **Right to Literacy:** The provision of essential educational resources and tools that empower individuals to navigate the digital world effectively.
- **Right to Exit:** The freedom to voluntarily leave a system, service, platform, organization, or protocol without facing excessive costs, penalties, or roadblocks.
- **Freedom of Mobility:** The liberty to freely move digital assets and identities across platforms, chains, or digital ecosystems.

### Archetypes
ZK Nation's primary archetypes are the **Outlaw** and the **Hero**. Secondary archetypes also provide some influence: the **Magician** highlights the magic of what zk tech makes possible and the **Jester** reflects a playful, approachable, and nimble attitude.

**Outlaw (Driven by Revolution).** The Outlaw is characterized by its rebellious nature, unafraid to challenge the status quo and disrupt established norms. Outlaws seek to create their own path, pushing boundaries and breaking rules to bring about change. They are daring and unapologetic in their approach, resonating with those who feel marginalized or left behind by conventional systems. ZK Nation embodies the Outlaw who seeks to subvert the status quo and support the revolutionary transformation that zk technology makes possible.
- *Tone of voice:* Disruptive, Rebellious, Combative, Defiant.
- *Strategy:* Challenging the status quo, Embracing nonconformity, Combative, Empowering the marginalized.
- *Goals & motivations:* Disruption, Revolution, Authenticity, Liberation, Nonconformity.
- *Fears:* Conformity, Insignificance, Powerlessness, Betrayal, Compliance.
- *Outlaw brands:* The Defiant, CryptoPunks, …
- *Tagline:* "Rules are made to be broken."

**Hero (Driven by Mastery).** Hero brands strive to make the world better by becoming the best version of themselves. Their world is defined by challenge, grit, and determination. The Hero redeems society by overcoming great odds through acts of strength, courage, and goodness. They are admired for the self-sacrifice and stamina required to triumph over adversity. There is a transformation arc to the Hero, which gives a grounded, pragmatic, and responsible energy to the rebelliousness of the Outlaw.
- *Tone of voice:* Confident, Honest, Candid, Brave.
- *Strategy:* Become stronger and better, Prove people wrong, Work hard and inspire action.
- *Goals & motivations:* Disruption, Revolution, Authenticity, Liberation, Nonconformity.

**Magician (Driven by Power).** Magician brands are all about bringing your wildest dreams to life. Their world is rooted in imagination and endless possibilities rather than reality and practicality. Magician brands thrive on creating awe-inspiring experiences and delivering innovative solutions that leave their audience spellbound. By tapping into deep knowledge, intuition, and expertise, these brands harness their creativity to manifest change and solve problems in unexpected ways. The Magician highlights that the technology behind ZK Nation is magical and powerful enough to completely transform and reshape our economic and societal structures.
- *Tone of voice:* Mysterious, Visionary, Imaginative, Captivating.
- *Strategy:* Cultivate a sense of wonder and mystery, Become a catalyst for positive change and growth, Inspire and captivate.

**Jester (Driven by Joy).** The Jester Brand Archetype thrives on humor, wit, and playfulness, aiming to entertain and spark joy. It challenges conventions, encouraging people to let go of inhibitions and embrace spontaneity. Through clever wordplay, puns, and creativity, Jester brands build emotional connections with audiences who appreciate a break from everyday seriousness. The Jester is playful, nimble, and quick-witted, responding rapidly to new memes and trends. This influence adds a touch of levity to the serious implications of our mission and technology.
- *Tone of voice:* Witty, Playful, Lighthearted, Irreverent.

- *Goals & motivations:* Fun, Laughter, Entertainment, Delight, Connection.

**Synthesis.** ZK Nation takes its mission seriously, but doesn't take itself too seriously. It is welcoming and empowering to a broad audience of people in crypto, who often have a rebellious streak within themselves. ZK Nation is an invitation for others to join the transformation arc from rebel to hero, with a bit of magic and fun along the way.

## ZK Credo (R2)

### Freedom → Progress → Prosperity
At regular intervals throughout history, new technological advancements have radically increased individual freedom, creating a flywheel that accelerates creativity, innovation, progress, and prosperity. We're in the midst of one of these transformations now.

As we advance through the Information Age, our structures of accounting, governance, record keeping and communication are all in need of a system upgrade. Humanity is at an inflection point. Tension between nation state powers is growing as accelerating artificial intelligence makes it increasingly difficult to tell what is real. The ways we share and store money and information need new support structures to survive accelerating technology and geopolitical instability.

In 2025, data leaks of information from China's great firewall have shown how its surveillance and censorship system is being exported to countries around the world.

In the same month, young people used Discord servers to overthrow their government in Nepal, and similar protests began forming in Morocco.

For many years authoritarian states have intentionally spread disinformation in open societies to foment chaos and instability, using the strengths and freedoms of those societies against them. Now, that freedom is under threat, but we have the technology to protect it.

For humanity to thrive in the coming decades, we need new methods to trust and verify digital information, to protect privacy, and to create resilient decentralized systems that protect and empower individual liberty as centralized systems of power continue to deteriorate.

Blockchains, Web 3, and zero knowledge cryptography constitute the Internet of Value. This is a new internet stack that is simultaneously open, private, and secure. This is civilizational technology, it can allow the technological, social and economic layers of society to work together and flourish. It creates promising new forms of societal organization, e.g. "network states", new digital nations that will carry humanity forward through our next stages of evolution.

The journey through the waves of the cryptographic revolution is ongoing. With public-key cryptography and blockchains marking the first and second waves, we now face the third: the ZK Revolution. Coupled with Web3, the ZK Revolution is set to redefine our collective future, standing as testament to technology's power to unlock personal freedom.

"ZK" stands for zero knowledge. Zero-knowledge cryptography allows one party to prove they know or possess certain information without ever revealing the information itself. On a practical level, this technology can be used to build societies that are simultaneously open, free, trustworthy, and secure.

In a voting system, ZK could let you prove that your vote was counted correctly without revealing who you voted for, protecting both privacy and election integrity at the same time.

On social media, ZK could let users prove they're real humans and not AI without ever sharing their personal data, enabling human verification without sacrificing anonymity or privacy.

In digital communication, ZK can ensure that messages are verified as authentic and untampered with, so you know who you're talking to, without exposing the content of the conversation to third parties, greatly enhancing privacy and security online.

In finance, ZK can allow users to prove they meet compliance rules (like KYC or creditworthiness) without revealing sensitive details such as account balances, identities, or transaction history.

ZK makes all of these feats possible through three important properties: Integrity, Privacy, and Magic.

### Integrity
*"Integrity is doing the right thing... even when no one else is looking or will ever know."*
ZK echoes the "don't trust, verify" ethos foundational to mathematics, open source, and blockchains. Computational integrity, enabled at any scale by recursive ZK proofs, is the cornerstone of this element.

### Privacy
*"Privacy is necessary for an open society in the electronic age."*
In the blockchain sphere, privacy, viewed as a fundamental right, poses challenges uniquely addressed by ZK. Privacy shouldn't be a gift given to us; it's a fundamental right we must assert and defend together.

### Magic
*"Any sufficiently advanced technology is indistinguishable from magic."*
ZK, endearingly dubbed "Magic Moon Math," is a marvel of technology. It makes the cumbersome simple, converting intricate operations into effortless clicks. It enables integrated systems, where components seamlessly synchronize. Above all, it weaves these wonders while honoring user privacy and control.

### The ZK Principles
We believe that to serve as the foundation of the Internet of Value, decentralized networks must adhere to the following principles:
- **Trustlessness.** Users must be able to verify the integrity of transactions and the network state independently, without relying on others.
- **Security.** An attack on any individual user must be as difficult and expensive as attacking the entire network, even for the world's most powerful actors.
- **Reliability.** The network must consistently and correctly perform its function without failure.
- **Censorship-Resistance.** Users must have the ability to transact on the network without needing permission from anyone.
- **Privacy.** Users must be able to protect their identities and transaction details. Sensitive information is not shared with others in the network without the consent of users.
- **Hyperscalability.** The network must have the capacity to grow with no upper bound while preserving all other critical properties.
- **Accessibility.** Applications and services on the network must be as affordable, easy-to-use and safe as state-of-the-art centralized alternatives.
- **Sovereignty.** Any group of users, even a minority, must have the right to exit — i.e., fork away from the network, while taking their assets with them at a minimal cost.

At present, Ethereum comes closest to realizing the vision of a blockchain network forming the backbone of the Internet of Value. It stands as a trustless, secure, reliable, censorship-resistant, and sovereign network. Thanks to ZK, privacy, hyperscalability, and accessibility are all possible on Ethereum.

Through ZK magic, Web3 on Ethereum is becoming a stronghold for privacy and achieving limitless scalability while maintaining integrity. In this transformative state — it will be an accessible and affordable sanctuary for digital sovereignty.

ZK will empower individuals globally, regardless of location. By unlocking these capabilities, a new wave of freedom, progress, and prosperity will impact lives globally.

### The Collective Action
ZK Principles empower a network where trust in operators is not needed to secure users' assets and control of their information. However, technology evolves and so do blockchains. The ZK Principles can't be fully safeguarded through technology alone.

To protect long term integrity, decentralization should be embraced. In practice, this means that each individual must have the power to exit a network and build it anew.

If a network's governance falls into the hands of a privileged few, it is destined to fail. The history of the modern Internet serves as a cautionary tale. Its inception promised decentralization, but over time user data and traffic fell into the control of a few tech giants, centralizing power and control and limiting the creativity and innovation possible online.

To avoid this fate, we believe that the ZK community must be fiercely sovereign by elevating the right to exit into a moral obligation. If the network deviates from its principles, network participants must unite and uphold these values by migrating to a new network.

Identifying such an erosion of values is not trivial: oppression is often subtle, slowly chipping away at freedom. Those in power may publicly punish dissenters to instill fear and encourage collective inaction.

Against these tactics, collective action is essential. ZK is protective technology, and the community too must protect minorities and celebrate those who bravely defy oppression. To embed this collective commitment deeply in the technology, and the people who use it, is vital to preserving freedom in the Internet of Value.

Realizing these principles requires time and perseverance. A steady, pragmatic approach to decentralization is needed. While short-term compromises might be made, the unwavering long-term vision remains: advancing personal freedom for all.

Through cryptography, we can build new systems of governance and communication that simultaneously allow individuals to voice their views while protecting their privacy. We can build new financial systems that give every individual access to the global economy.
