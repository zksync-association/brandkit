ACCENT & ADDITIONAL

Usage rules & accessibility
  • Default to light surfaces (Neutral-50 / Brand-25) or a subtle gradient.
  • Brand-700 = accent (headlines, links, actions), never a full-bleed background.
  • Large fills step down to Brand-300/400 or neutrals.
  • Salmon is a sparing governance accent — one band/highlight, never body text.
  • Body text: Neutral-950 or Brand-900; avoid Brand-300/400 for copy.
  • Meet WCAG AA contrast.

LOGO & MARKS

Always use the original logomark — never redraw it.

Clear space & the "never" list

Keep ≥1× clear space (≥2× small). The ZKsync wordmark is the master brand; the ZK Nation square logomark (blue default, orange expressive) is the governance sub-brand. Use the original artwork.

  • No stretch, rotate, skew, recolor, or badging.
  • Don't rebuild from dots/pixels/patterns.
  • Don't pair with unapproved type or busy imagery.
