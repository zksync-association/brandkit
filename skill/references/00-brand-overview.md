# Brand Overview & Architecture

> Sources: ZKsync Visual Guideline v.2025.2; ZK Nation brand exploration (Jan 2026);
> zknation.io; docs.zknation.io (Mission — ZK Credo, Governance North Star).

## Who this is

The **ZKsync Association** is the legally recognized non-profit that helps secure,
defend, and grow the **ZKsync protocol**. Its community-facing governance identity is
**ZK Nation** — "a community driven by a shared purpose" that "unites chains, builders,
and operators to govern and grow the ZKsync Protocol."

## Brand architecture (two layers)

This brand kit covers two layers. **Default to ZK Nation** — for this kit it is the **primary**
brand. The **ZKsync protocol** brand (zksync.io) is the **secondary** option, used only when the
work is explicitly about the protocol/technology/enterprise product (see
`secondary-brand-zksync-protocol.md`). Architecturally ZKsync is the master brand and ZK Nation
its governance expression, but in day-to-day use here, reach for ZK Nation first.

| | **ZKsync** (master brand) | **ZK Nation** (governance sub-brand) |
|---|---|---|
| Used for | The protocol, product UI, technical/developer surfaces | Governance, community, voting, delegation, the DAO |
| Logo | Double-arrow logomark + `ZKsync` wordmark | Same mark + `ZK Nation` lockup (blue or orange) |
| Display type | **Inter** | **ES Allianz** (editorial headlines) |
| UI / labels | Inter | **Avenue Mono** (uppercase mono nav, buttons, captions) |
| Accent | Brand-700 blue | Brand-700 blue **+ salmon** (`#EE6D50`), used sparingly |
| Feel | Light, airy, precise, institutional-grade | The same, plus a civic / "signal-flag" expressiveness |

Both layers share **one color system, one logomark, and one set of principles.** The
sub-brand differs mainly in typographic voice (ES Allianz + Avenue Mono) and the salmon
accent. When in doubt, default to the master-brand rules.

## Mission — the ZK Credo

> **Freedom → Progress → Prosperity**

ZK Nation exists to embed the values of the **ZK Credo** — *freedom, progress, and
prosperity* — into every layer of the ZKsync protocol. "History shows us how technology
can expand personal freedoms, unleashing creativity and innovation, leading to progress
and prosperity."

### "ZK" stands for three properties
- **Integrity** — "don't trust, verify." Computational integrity at any scale via recursive ZK proofs.
- **Privacy** — "a fundamental right we must assert and defend together."
- **Magic** — "any sufficiently advanced technology is indistinguishable from magic." ("Magic Moon Math.")

### The ZK Principles (the network must be)
Trustlessness · Security · Reliability · Censorship-Resistance · Privacy ·
Hyperscalability · Accessibility · Sovereignty (the right to exit).

### Governance North Star
The governance system exists to (1) **secure the ZKsync protocol** by decentralizing
control, and (2) **activate programs in service of the ZK Credo.** Primary goals:
Secure the Protocol · Expand Onchain Assets · Increase Active Builders · Strengthen the ZK Community.

## The three governance bodies
Power is balanced across three bodies — the **Token Assembly**, the **Guardians**, and the
**Security Council** — under a legally recognized non-profit structure (MiCAR-compliant).

## How to use this kit
1. Decide the layer: **ZKsync** (product/technical) or **ZK Nation** (governance/community).
2. Pull tokens from `assets/tokens/` (CSS / Tailwind / JSON — one source of truth).
3. Follow the relevant reference file (color, typography, logo, visual-language, components, voice).
   For positioning, mission/vision, brand values, archetypes, and the four voice traits, see
   `verbal-identity.md` (the canonical Brand House) and `voice-tone-vocabulary.md`.
4. Use assets from `assets/` (logos, icons, flags, fonts) — never recreate the mark.
