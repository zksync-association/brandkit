## Voice — the four traits

ZK Nation **takes its mission seriously, but doesn't take itself too seriously.** We sound
authoritative yet inclusive, technical yet accessible — a builder-led, principled community making
serious infrastructure feel open and human. Our personality combines revolutionary, ambitious
innovation with grounded focus and quick wit. We speak in a way that is **Bold, Grounded, Dynamic,
and Passionate** — the four canonical voice traits (each rooted in a brand value; full detail in
[[verbal-identity]]):

1. **Bold** *(root: Freedom).* Confident and courageous about our values; unafraid to take a strong
   stance and set the tone for the industry. Loud and confident enough to break through the noise of
   scams and short-term players. Defend open-source values, privacy, and individual sovereignty at
   every opportunity. *"Don't trust, verify."*
2. **Grounded** *(root: Responsibility).* Measured, pragmatic, resourceful, meritocratic — building
   for the endgame. Integrity and alignment between words and actions; clear roadmaps; proactively
   own mistakes. When we report, we ground claims in outcomes (KPIs, dates, addresses), not
   adjectives, and name incidents plainly rather than spin them.
3. **Dynamic** *(root: Constant evolution).* At the cutting edge; take the lead and set the pace with
   quick wit. Articulate clear visions of what ZK makes possible; fold in the memes and narratives of
   the moment, lightly. Energetic and adaptable.
4. **Passionate** *(root: Serious play).* Passion is infectious. We play long-term games with
   long-term players — cooperative competition that brings out the best. Balance serious ambition
   with playful camaraderie. *"Shape the Future of ZKsync, Together."*

**Constants across all four:** the subject is usually "we," "the community," "together"; sentences are
short and declarative; concrete nouns (delegate, proposal, quorum, Capped Minter) over vague ones
(synergy, solution); technical terms used correctly, never as decoration. Anchor claims in the ZK
Credo (freedom, progress, prosperity). No exclamation marks, no urgency theatre — confidence comes
from clarity. *"Privacy shouldn't be a gift given to us; it's a fundamental right we must assert and
defend together."*

## Tone — shifts by context

| Context | Dial toward | Example register |
|---|---|---|
| Mission / manifesto | Visionary, historic, resolute | "We're at the dawn of a new era… Let us remain resolute in championing digital self-ownership. Onward." |
| Governance & docs | Neutral, exact, procedural | "Proposals should aim to achieve goals supporting the vision of the ZK Credo." |
| Operational / report | Measured, factual, accountable | "The Security Council successfully executed two Emergency Upgrades during the validation period." |
| Legal / disclaimer | Sober, careful, hedged | "Any forward-looking statements are inherently uncertain and subject to change… the Association disclaims all liability." |
| Product / UI | Plain, instructional, terse | `CONNECT WALLET`, `VOTE`, "Authenticate ZKsync Governance" |
| Community / social | Warm, direct, invitational | "New to ZKsync? Learn more here." |
| Security | Sober, careful, unambiguous | "Audited and reviewed… Irreversible and conclusive." |

Across all contexts: **no hype, no FOMO, no price talk, no emoji spam.** Confidence comes
from clarity. The report register is the longest-form proof of this: paragraphs of plain
past-tense fact, incidents named openly, claims tied to evidence, and a single evocative
word ("Foundations," "Onward") to open a section rather than a slogan.

## Vocabulary — say / don't say

**Spelling & casing (get these exactly right):**
- **ZKsync** — one word, capital `ZK`, lowercase `sync`. *Not* "zkSync", "ZK Sync",
  "Zksync", or "ZKSync". (Historical material may show "zkSync"; current brand is **ZKsync**.)
- **ZK Nation** — two words, both capitalized.
- **ZKsync Association** — the non-profit entity.
- **ZK token** / **ZK** — the governance token. Lowercase "token."
- **ZK Stack**, **Elastic Network**, **ZK Chain(s)**, **Bridgehub**, **ZKsync Gateway**,
  **ZKsync Atlas** — capitalized as shown.
- **ZK Credo** — capitalized. The values: *freedom, progress, prosperity* (lowercase in prose).
- **onchain** — one word, lowercase. **Web3**, **ZK Revolution** — as shown.

**Preferred terms:** govern · delegate · proposal · quorum · steward · sovereignty ·
interoperability · trustless · verifiable · censorship-resistant · the Elastic Network ·
the community · builders, operators, and chains.

**Governance & token mechanics (cite exactly as the report does):**
- **Token Assembly · Guardians · Security Council** — the three balancing governance bodies.
- **Emergency Upgrade Board · Protocol Upgrade Handler** — the emergency-response actors/contracts.
- **ZIP** (ZKsync Improvement Proposal) · **TPP** (Token Program Proposal) · **GAP** (Governance
  Advisory Proposal) — the three proposal types. Spell out on first use, then the acronym.
- **Token Program** · **Capped Minter** · **Minter Mod** · **mint-on-demand** /
  **"just-in-time minting"** — the token-mechanics vocabulary. The brand framing is
  *"mechanics over multisigs."*
- **ZKGPS** (ZKsync Governance Program Services Foundation) · **ZKsync Foundation** — affiliated entities.

**Avoid:** "crypto bro" hype ("to the moon," "wagmi," "LFG"); "decentralized" used loosely
without a mechanism; "revolutionary/game-changing" as filler; calling the token an
"investment" or referencing price/returns; "users" when "the community" or "delegates" is meant.

## Glossary (one-liners)
- **ZK Nation** — community that governs, defends, and grows the ZKsync protocol.
- **ZKsync Chain** — fully customizable autonomous rollup, validium, or volition built on ZK Stack.
- **ZK Stack** — modular, developer-friendly framework to deploy interoperable ZK-powered chains.
- **Elastic Network** — network of ZKsync Chains connected through a single Bridgehub, natively interoperable.
- **ZKsync Gateway** — shared settlement layer for ZK Chains; lowers costs and enables fast, unified proof aggregation.
- **ZKsync Atlas** — the November 2025 protocol upgrade delivering a unified security and interoperability model.
- **Token Assembly / Guardians / Security Council** — the three balancing governance bodies.
- **Delegate** — a community member who votes on proposals on behalf of token holders.
- **ZIP / TPP / GAP** — Protocol Improvement, Token Program, and Governance Advisory proposals.
- **Capped Minter** — a smart contract granted a maximum supply it may mint on demand ("just-in-time minting"), removing the risk of a large standing treasury.
- **Minter Mod** — modular smart-contract logic (rate limits, eligibility, delays, triggers) chained onto a Capped Minter to shape minting rights.
- **Emergency Upgrade Board** — the body that coordinates and executes emergency protocol upgrades under predefined procedures.
- **ZKGPS** — ZKsync Governance Program Services Foundation; contracts with vetted service providers on behalf of the Token Assembly.
